# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use multiple large-language-model providers from an Android phone and need a native, efficient chat client with configurable assistants, tools, and model settings.

## Product Purpose

RikkaHub provides a native Android interface for everyday AI conversations across multiple providers. Success means users can start, resume, organize, and manage chats quickly while understanding account and model state at the point of use.

## Brand Personality

Native, capable, and direct. The interface should feel familiar to Android users, quiet during focused work, and technically trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style composition inside the app, ornamental visual effects, unfamiliar replacements for standard Android controls, and broad visual rewrites that disrupt established workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the conversation workflow primary.
2. Surface operational state where it affects the next action.
3. Prefer familiar Material interaction patterns.
4. Preserve user control over providers, assistants, and data.
5. Make focused changes that remain consistent with the existing product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect system font scaling, light and dark themes, dynamic color, semantic labels, and standard touch-target behavior. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
